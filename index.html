What patientsaskfirst.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as an intense workout: strong muscle contractions and gentle warmth. Intensity is set for your comfort.

How many sessions will I need?

A typical plan is 4 sessions, spaced 5 to 10 days apart. Dr. Kroner personalizes it to your goal.

Is there downtime?

None. You lie down fully clothed for about 30 minutes and head straight back to your day.

When will I notice results?

Effects keep developing for up to 3 months after your final session.

What happens at the $199 visit?

A sports medicine exam with Dr. Kroner, a personal plan and a mini Emsculpt NEO demo so you can feel it.

Is it only for athletes?

No. Ridgeview offers the same technology to weekend warriors, professional athletes and everyone in between.

Who should not have it?

It is not for people with metal or electronic implants such as pacemakers, or during pregnancy. Dr. Kroner screens every patient first.

Yes. Ridgeview DPC members save on all Regen services. Ask about membership at your visit.
